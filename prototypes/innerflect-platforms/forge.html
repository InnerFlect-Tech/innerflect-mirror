Questions

Before you build.

Are these finished website templates?

They are more flexible than templates: complete prompts and system specifications covering structure, art direction, behaviour, states and acceptance criteria.

Can I use the results commercially?

Commercial use is included where shown. Third-party references are documented as research provenance and their code or assets are not redistributed.

Can Forge systems connect to Innerflect OS?

Yes. Composer can combine a customer-facing Forge experience with an OS automation, agent or operating blueprint.
