Questions

Before you automate.

Are these no-code automation templates?

No. Each product is an implementation system covering the workflow, prompts, data contracts, governance, states, integrations and evaluation—not only a connector diagram.

Which AI platform is required?

Products declare their compatibility and dependencies. The architecture stays portable where possible, and provider-specific requirements are explicit.

Can OS power a Forge website?

Yes. A Forge experience can connect to an OS operating layer for qualification, research, knowledge retrieval, approvals or CRM handoff.
